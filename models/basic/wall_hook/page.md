## Lower mounting screw access

The lower Ø4.2 mm mounting hole passes only through the 6 mm rear mounting
spine. A separate Ø8 mm opening through the diagonal reinforcement gives a
straight screwdriver/bit path to the lower screw while keeping the mounting
wall intact.


## Technical drawing

The Wall Hook publishes a native SolidCraft **A4 landscape** engineering sheet
at 2:1 scale. The sheet carries the principal manufacturing and mounting
dimensions, a shaded isometric view and native PDF/PNG drawing artifacts. Hole
centre positions are stated as a drawing note because the pinned SolidCraft
drawing command set does not expose qualified centre-mark/centre-line authoring.
