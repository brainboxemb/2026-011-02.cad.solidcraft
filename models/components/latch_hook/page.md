## Source and manufacturing intent

This component is reconstructed from the supplied native SolidCraft
`hook.sc` / `hook.json` pair. Those files are stored under `reference/`.

The manufacturing history is intentionally identical in structure to the
native source: one flat sheet blank, two mounting holes and one sheet fold.

The drawing dimensions **15 mm** and **20 mm** are not competing values. The
15 mm dimension is the neck width in the top view; 20 mm is the total formed
projection in the side view.

The outer contour is no longer inferred from the drawing. Its exact arc and
line endpoints come from the supplied native sketch.
