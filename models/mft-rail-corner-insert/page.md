## Role in the MFT corner

This is the loose protective/filler piece at the exposed outside corner of the
two mounting rails. It is deliberately separate from the structural
MFT Rail Corner Bracket.

That split matches the physical design better:

- the bracket carries rail and worktop loads;
- the insert fills and rounds the exposed outer corner;
- the two integral clip lips retain the insert against the rail corner;
- the insert can be replaced without replacing the structural bracket.

The exact geometry is sourced from the native `hoek_assembly` reference kept
with the MFT Rail Corner Assembly.
