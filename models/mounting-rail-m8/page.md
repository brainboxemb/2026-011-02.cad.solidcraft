## Mounting rail

Generic galvanized M8 mounting rail, modelled as a **27 × 18 × 1.25 mm**
C-profile. The standalone CAD example is **250 mm** long; the retail reference
product is sold as a 1 metre rail.

This type of light installation rail is also commonly seen at originally
German DIY/building-supply chains in the Netherlands. Its profile and punch
layout resemble the **MÜPRO MPC 27/18 system**.

## Repeating hole pattern

One **100 mm** repeat consists of:

- **Ø12.5 mm** round hole;
- **10 × 6.4 mm** longitudinal slot;
- **Ø12.5 mm** round hole;
- **15 × 8.4 mm** transverse slot.

The four feature centres are **25 mm** apart. The model repeats this complete
four-feature unit as one native pattern.
