MFT Rail Corner Assembly

Two perpendicular Mounting Rail M8 profiles with structural bracket and replaceable PETG corner insert

Built in SolidCraft 0.50.0 130 commands

Model preview

Native-source MFT rail corner subassembly. Two 150 mm MountingRailM8 pieces meet at 90 degrees with their C-openings facing outward. The structural PETG bracket fixes and supports both rail ends. A separate replaceable PETG corner insert fills the exposed outside corner and clips into place with two integral retaining lips. The bracket rail holes align with the first and second round perforations, while the top gusset exposes the MFT underside screw seat.

Key dimensions: 2 × 150 mm rails; 75 mm bracket arms; 6 mm under-rail feet; exact R18 bracket foot corner; separate PETG corner insert; rail holes at 12.5 / 62.5 mm

Construction stages

  • X rail — 150 mm MountingRailM8, opening toward -Y
  • Y rail — 150 mm MountingRailM8, opening toward -X
  • Bracket — place the reusable MFT Rail Corner Bracket at the common inside corner
  • Alignment — use existing round perforations for both bracket fixing pairs
  • Rail support — rest both rail ends on the 6 mm printed feet
  • Corner insert — place the separate PETG filler/protection part at the exposed outside corner
  • Clip retention — use the two source-derived insert lips against the rail corner
  • Access — keep both C-openings and rail ends free for slide-in hardware

Model context

Background

Qualification purpose

This model deliberately stays separate from the full Simple MFT assembly while the bracket dimensions are still provisional.

The two short rail pieces reproduce the same corner orientation used on the MFT support frame:

  • one rail runs along +X with its C-opening toward −Y;
  • one rail runs along +Y with its C-opening toward −X;
  • both rail back webs meet at the inside corner;
  • the bracket occupies the +X/+Y inside quadrant;
  • the top bracket face is flush with the future MFT underside plane at Z=0.

The first two round rail perforations are used as the bracket fixing datum: 12.5 mm and 62.5 mm from each rail end.

The MFT underside screw position and all non-rail bracket dimensions remain provisional until the real worktop/hardware are measured.

Load-bearing foot and separate corner insert

The prototype bracket now continues underneath both rails. Each 75 mm rail end rests on a 6 mm printed shoe. This gives the corner bracket a second function: it becomes a small height-adjusting/support foot rather than only a side clamp.

The structural bracket still carries both rail ends on 6 mm printed support feet, but the supplied native source refines the outside corner in two ways.

First, the bracket’s own lower corner is an exact R18 quarter-round sector, rather than the earlier segmented R22 approximation.

Second, the visible outside-corner filler is now a separate physical part: MFT Rail Corner Insert. The insert has its own R18 outer arc, a source-shaped inner relief and two integral PETG clip lips with sloped lead-ins. This makes the exposed corner protection independently printable and replaceable while the structural bracket remains unchanged in service.

Native assembly reference

The supplied complete native model is preserved with this subassembly:

  • reference/hoek_assembly.sc;
  • reference/hoek_assembly.json.

Those files describe the complete rail/bracket/insert corner and are kept here rather than duplicated under the individual component owners.

"""Qualification assembly for the MFT rail corner bracket.

Two short MountingRailM8 pieces meet at 90 degrees with their C-openings facing
outward. The bracket occupies only the inside corner and uses the first two
round perforations on each rail.
"""

from scpf import Placement

from models.components.mft_rail_corner_bracket.scpf.model import (
    MFTRailCornerBracket,
)
from models.components.mft_rail_corner_insert.scpf.model import (
    MFTRailCornerInsert,
)
from models.components.mounting_rail_m8.scpf.model import MountingRailM8


class MFTRailCornerAssembly:
    rail_length = 150.0
    rail_repeat_count = 1
    rail_repeat_pitch = 100.0

    RAIL_Z = -MountingRailM8.PROFILE_WIDTH / 2

    # Same orientation contracts already used by the Simple MFT frame:
    # X rail: length +X, opening -Y.
    X_RAIL_ROTATION = ((1, 1, -1), 120)
    # Y rail: length +Y, opening -X.
    Y_RAIL_ROTATION = ((0, -1, 0), 90)

    def build(self):
        x_rail = MountingRailM8(
            length=self.rail_length,
            repeat_count=self.rail_repeat_count,
            repeat_pitch=self.rail_repeat_pitch,
            placement=Placement(
                x=0,
                y=0,
                z=self.RAIL_Z,
                rotation=self.X_RAIL_ROTATION,
            ),
        )

        y_rail = MountingRailM8(
            length=self.rail_length,
            repeat_count=self.rail_repeat_count,
            repeat_pitch=self.rail_repeat_pitch,
            placement=Placement(
                x=0,
                y=0,
                z=self.RAIL_Z,
                rotation=self.Y_RAIL_ROTATION,
            ),
        )

        bracket = MFTRailCornerBracket()
        corner_insert = MFTRailCornerInsert()

        return x_rail + y_rail + bracket + corner_insert
