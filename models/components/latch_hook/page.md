## Manufacturing intent

This hook is intentionally modeled as one native sheet-metal part rather than
as a solid block with a decorative curved end. The CAD history follows the
manufacturing sequence directly: flat blank, two mounting holes, then the hook
bend.

Only the dimensioned values from the supplied drawing are treated as exact.
The undimensioned outer mounting-lobe transition is resolved by tangency from
the dimensioned width, hole pitch and edge datum.
