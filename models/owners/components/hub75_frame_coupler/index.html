HUB75 Frame Coupler

Configured frame-coupler family with standard and tube-clamp interfaces

10 variants Physical owner components/hub75_frame_coupler

Model family

Choose a variant

Reusable HUB75 frame-coupler family with native Small, Medium and Large configuration rows; Medium is the default. Standard seam/edge/corner members share one source-derived parameter graph with two tube-interface families: protruding rails for the female-slot clamp and female receivers for the male-dovetail clamp. Native reference ticks and blind tapered reference holes remain independently selectable creation options on every reusable coupler, but both are disabled by default to keep routine Forge/SolidCraft builds small.
