## Design context

The HUB75 enclosure notes collected several 3D-printable hinge directions:
simple stop/180-degree hinges, concealed multi-link hinges and spider-type
mechanisms. This model starts with the mechanically simplest 180-degree
external hinge so the enclosure mounting interface, pin diameter and print
clearances can be qualified first.

The 36 mm reference scale comes from the earlier “180° Strong Hinge, 36 mm”
concept entry. The geometry here is newly authored and intentionally generic;
it does not copy that reference model.

A concealed or spider mechanism can later become a second Display hinge
variant once the physical enclosure establishes the required offset and
clearance motion.
