A compact two-leaf hinge reconstructed from the supplied SolidCraft design.

Both leaves use the same 40 × 30 × 3 mm plate and Ø8 × 40 mm knuckle geometry.
The hinge opens around the shared X-axis and the supplied design starts at
**60°**, with a permitted range of **0° to 180°**.

The generated model is intended to retain that movement as a real SolidCraft
revolute joint rather than as a fixed rotated copy.
