WARNING — measured/reference geometry, not an official dimensional specification

The Makita cross-section is based on a third-party STEP reference and calibrated to the 182.50 × 10.28 mm envelope measured by TSO Products. It has not been independently verified against a physical rail or an official Makita extrusion drawing. Treat this model as an assembly/layout reference, not as verified manufacturing geometry or authoritative Makita dimensions.

Model preview

A second fixed cross-section of the generic GuideRail component. The aluminium shape comes from the Makita 199141-8 STEP reference and is calibrated to the TSO measured 182.50 × 10.28 mm envelope. The standalone CAD example uses 600 mm for compact visual inspection.

Key dimensions: 182.50 × 10.28 mm profile; 600 mm standalone example

Construction stages

  • Select the fixed Makita cross-section at build time
  • Create one detailed XZ Polygon2D
  • Approximate the STEP's small round edges with short line segments
  • Extrude symmetrically along Y to the 600 mm example length
