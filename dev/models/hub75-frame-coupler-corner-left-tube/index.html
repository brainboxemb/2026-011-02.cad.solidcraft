HUB75 Frame Coupler — Corner Left Tube

Medium left Corner coupler with one loose-clamp slide interface

Built in SolidCraft 0.49.0 394 commands

Model preview

Tube-ready derivative of the accepted Medium left Corner coupler. It adds the source insertion recess, Ø10.5 tube clearance, one slide rail and its asymmetric entry chamfer.

Key dimensions: Medium; local rail center +7 mm; Ø10.5 tube clearance; 14.75 mm rail height

  • Add the source slide rail
